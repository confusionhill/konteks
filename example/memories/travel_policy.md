# Corporate Travel & Booking Policy

This document establishes the official guidelines for flight bookings, hotel accommodations, and activity expenses.

## 1. Hotel Accommodation Guidelines
- **Maximum Nightly Rate Caps**:
  - **Tokyo**: $150 USD / night
  - **Paris**: $200 USD / night
  - **Bali**: $120 USD / night
  - **New York**: $250 USD / night
- **Required Amenities**:
  - High-speed Wi-Fi is mandatory for all business travel bookings.
  - Swimming pool access is preferred for executive tier guests.

## 2. Flight Booking Rules
- Economy or Economy Plus class is standard for flights under 8 hours.
- Direct flights are prioritized unless layovers offer savings greater than 30%.
- Preferred Partner Airlines: **Japan Airlines (JL)**, **Air France (AF)**, **Singapore Airlines (SQ)**.

## 3. Activities & Expenses
- Maximum daily activity allowance per traveler is **$100 USD**.
- Guided cultural tours and sightseeing excursions require pre-approval if exceeding $80.
