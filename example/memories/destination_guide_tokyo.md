# Tokyo Destination & Neighborhood Guide

An operational guide for travel assistants organizing itineraries in Tokyo.

## Neighborhood Breakdown

### 1. Shinjuku & Shibuya (Central & Vibrant)
- Ideal for travelers seeking nightlife, shopping, and major transport hubs.
- Recommended Hotels: *Tokyo Grand Palace*, *Shinjuku Capsule Pods*.
- Key Activities: *Shibuya Crossing & Izakaya Crawl*.

### 2. Asakusa & Ueno (Cultural & Traditional)
- Best for historic sights, temples, and tranquil traditional stays.
- Recommended Hotel: *Asakusa Traditional Ryokan*.

### 3. Akihabara (Tech & Pop Culture)
- Famous for electronics, anime, and gaming culture.
- Key Activities: *Akihabara Anime & Gaming Tour*.
